Who's behind the handle

I'm xivi, a reverse engineer. I'm interested in how software works under the hood: from packers and protections to network protocols and firmware.

I work at the intersection of binary analysis and practical security: I find vulnerabilities, analyze suspicious samples, write tools that save hours of manual work, and do cracking and analysis of how various game systems work.

I solve challenges on crackmes.one, reversing.kr, and pwn.college
I publish their solutions and detailed writeups on my GitHub
